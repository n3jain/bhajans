Why this notebook exists

This is my personal bhajan diary, opened up online so it is easier to carry, search, and enjoy during kirtans.

In many kirtan gatherings in India, people keep handwritten notebooks of bhajans. Songs are learned from family, friends, temples, satsangs, and other singers, and many have been passed along across generations. This notebook is a digital continuation of that tradition.

I have not written these bhajans and I do not claim authorship of them. Wherever I can identify the original author, poet, composer, performer, or source, I try to preserve that attribution.

If you use a bhajan from this notebook professionally or publish it elsewhere, please credit the original author or rights holder wherever known and verify the appropriate permissions for your use.

I do not endorse plagiarism or unauthorized use. The purpose of this site is personal and community enjoyment of bhajans, especially in kirtan—not to claim ownership of someone else’s creative work.

If you notice an attribution that is missing or incorrect, please treat the source information in the notebook as something to verify rather than as a claim of ownership.
